# My Reflection on ICT

Before learning about ICT, I mostly thought of it as using computers, the internet, and other digital technologies. However, I now understand that ICT is much more than that. It is a way of connecting people, sharing information, learning new things, and solving problems through technology.

For me, ICT has become an important part of my daily life. I use technology for studying, communicating with others, finding information, and working on different projects. It has made learning more convenient because I can access information and educational resources whenever I need them.

As a Mechatronics Engineering student, I also see ICT as an important part of my future. Engineering is becoming increasingly connected with computers, automation, programming, and smart technologies. I believe that improving my ICT skills will help me become more confident in my studies and better prepared for my future career.

Overall, ICT means **opportunity and growth** to me. It helps me learn, express my ideas, connect with others, and prepare for the future. I believe that learning how to use technology effectively is not only useful for my education but also an important skill for my personal and professional development.
